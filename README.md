## About Me

I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science & Engineering**, working as a Research Assistant at the **University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)** in the Radiation Oncology Lab under **Prof. Sharon Qi**. My research centers on building machine learning systems for early cancer detection — translating clinical imaging and patient data into models that support oncologists in making more accurate, earlier diagnoses.

My academic path spans three countries and three research groups, each contributing a different lens to the problem of AI in oncology.

---

## Research

### 🏛️ UCLA — Radiation Oncology Lab *(Current)*
**Supervisor:** Prof. Sharon Qi · University of California, Los Angeles

Building early detection and predictive models for **Lung, Breast, Head and Neck cancers**, and **Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)** using machine learning. Work involves clinical imaging data, radiomics feature extraction, and model interpretability for clinical deployment.

### 🌏 University of Western Australia
**Supervisor:** Prof. Dr. Martin Ebert · in collaboration with Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, Perth

Cancer classification using AI algorithms on real clinical datasets. Research focused on bridging the gap between academic ML methods and hospital-grade reliability requirements.

### 🇧🇩 United International University
**Supervisor:** Prof. Dr. Nomani Kabir

Predictive modeling for lung and breast cancer using classical and ensemble ML approaches. Foundation for understanding clinical feature engineering and survival analysis.

---

## Research Interests

- **Cancer Detection & Classification** — Lung, Breast, Head & Neck, TNBC
- **Medical Image Analysis** — CT/MRI segmentation, radiomics, deep learning on imaging data
- **Predictive & Survival Modeling** — Cox regression, Kaplan-Meier, multi-feature pipelines
- **Explainable AI in Healthcare** — Building models clinicians can understand and trust
- **Multi-modal Data Fusion** — Combining imaging, genomic, and EHR features
