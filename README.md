## 🧑‍🔬 About Me

I am **AL Imran**, an AI researcher and emerging computer scientist with a focus on the **algorithmic and theoretical foundations of machine learning** — particularly multimodal representation learning, optimization in deep models, and principled interpretability applied to biomedical data.

I work simultaneously across three research institutions:

- **UCLA** — Research Assistant under [Prof. Dr. Sharon Qi, PhD, DABR](https://bioscience.ucla.edu/people/xiangrong-qi/), developing survival prediction frameworks for cancer patients using DICOM imaging and clinical data
- **BUET** — Lead Researcher under Prof. Dr. M. Sohel Rahman(https://msrahman.buet.ac.bd/), designing explainable multimodal AI for TNBC and lung cancer
- **UWA, Australia** — AI Research Assistant under Prof. Dr. Martin Ebert, building reproducible medical imaging AI with Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital

I use biomedical imaging and clinical datasets as a stress test for ML theory — exposing challenges in inductive bias, optimization stability, and representation collapse that arise in real-world learning systems.

> *"I aim to advance principled machine learning methodologies grounded in algorithm design, optimization theory, and representation learning that extend beyond domain-specific applications and contribute to the foundations of intelligent systems."*

## 🔬 Research Areas

### 🧬 Computational Biology & Bioinformatics
Multi-omics integration (RNA-seq, proteomics, clinical variables), genomic survival modeling, biological sequence analysis, and principled ML on high-dimensional sparse biological data. Building pipelines from raw sequencing to clinically actionable predictions.

### 🏥 AI for Cancer Detection & Oncology
Early detection and prognosis models for **Lung, Breast (Histopathology, TNBC), Head & Neck cancers**. Radiomics feature extraction from DICOM CT/MRI scans using NIH TCIA datasets. Reusable CNN pipelines integrating imaging + clinical CSV for scalable prognostic modeling.

### 🤖 Large Language Models (LLMs) in Clinical Medicine
Fine-tuning and evaluating biomedical LLMs (BioMedLM, ClinicalBERT, LLaMA) on radiology reports, oncology notes, and medical literature. Structured information extraction from unstructured EHR text for downstream prediction tasks.

### 👁️ Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for Medical Imaging
Multimodal models (CLIP, Med-PaLM, LLaVA-Med) aligning radiology images with clinical language. Cross-modal reasoning across pathology slides, CT scans, and clinical text for zero/few-shot diagnosis.

### 🫀 AI Integration into Human Biology & Physiology
End-to-end modeling of biological systems: physiological signal processing (ECG, EEG), modality-aware fusion operators for radiomic + clinical variable alignment, and constraint-driven explainability as a formal learning objective.

### 📊 Explainable & Trustworthy AI (XAI)
SHAP, LIME, Grad-CAM, and attention visualization integrated as learning constraints — not post-hoc additions. Sparsity-inducing and information-theoretic regularizers that promote semantically meaningful latent structures.
